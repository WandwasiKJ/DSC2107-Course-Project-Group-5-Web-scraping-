Key Conclusions & Data Limitations

  • Price: Mobile Phones have the highest median price (510,000 UGX), while Smart Watches are the most relatively variable. Outliers are present and should be manually reviewed.
  • Rankings: Listing ranks fluctuate significantly (up to 192 positions), suggesting high competition and dynamic visibility, not necessarily a direct measure of sales.
  • Brands: Based on title analysis, Apple, Samsung, and Tecno are prominent brands in the Mobile Phones category. Xiaomi has a significant presence in Smart Watches.
  • Discounts: The discount data is severely limited and cannot be used to make broad conclusions about platform-wide discounting behavior.
  • Missing Data: Ratings, review counts, and seller names were not scraped, so these critical factors could not be analyzed.
  • Timeframe: The analysis covers 6 dates, not the 7-day period the assignment may have required.
